ABOUT JIKŌ

What is 時光?

JIKŌ is not a system for labeling people as good or bad.

It is an original symbolic reading system created in Japan. It maps twelve innate aspects of your nature and looks at how your inner light moves through them.

The system draws on multiple traditions and symbolic languages, while forming its own modern framework.
