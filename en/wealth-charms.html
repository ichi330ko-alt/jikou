Prosperity Talismans 豊かさの護符

Prosperity is not only about having more money. It can also mean protecting what already supports your life, cultivating what you already possess, and allowing what you truly need to reach you. These JIKŌ talismans are designed around those different movements of abundance.

Each JIKŌ talisman brings together symbolic functions of prosperity into a single design. The characters and symbols express how abundance is invited, received, cultivated, protected, or circulated. These fixed talismans are different from personalized talismans, which are created from an individual's Prosperity Flow Reading.

A completed JIKŌ talisman for supporting a stable everyday life through a three-part flow: inviting what is needed, allowing it to fill and nourish your life, and protecting the foundations that support you.

What makes this different?

These are not shrine-issued omamori and are not presented as traditional Japanese religious objects. They are original symbolic talismans created within JIKŌ, a contemporary reading system developed in Japan. Japanese characters are used because each character carries a specific function within the system.
