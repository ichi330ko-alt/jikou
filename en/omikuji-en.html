月御籤 Tsuki Omikuji

The moon waxes, wanes, and returns.
Receive one message reflected in the phase of your present moment.

This is not meant to choose an answer for you.
Hold one matter quietly in mind, then touch the moon and receive today’s oracle.

One draw per day. The same oracle remains open until the date changes.
